FAQ

Briefly, what people ask before a deal

Ask your question
Is this a primary sale?

Yes. Available units are sold directly by the developer in all three complexes, including the completed U1 and U2

Is “from 10%” before tax?

Yes: a benchmark before tax and before maintenance downtime, based on the operating U1 and U2. We will send an exact model for your unit on request

Can I resell before handover?

Yes, for U3, which is under construction. Terms are in the agreement

What does the management company do?

Check-in, cleaning, maintenance, booking channels, reporting. The fee is a percentage of income

When is U3 handed over?

Autumn 2026. We publish dated construction progress

Which currency?

We accept different currencies settled in USD; instalments available. Exact terms are agreed at the deal

Leasehold or freehold?

Depends on the unit and the deal structure. Covered in the legal pack before signing

How does the deal go?

Directly with Unit Space, documents signed electronically via DocuSign. The full process is in “How to buy”

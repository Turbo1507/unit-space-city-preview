Living with nature

The city is built into the landscape. You walk among rice fields and jungle every day, with every city convenience close at hand

Everything close by

36 cafes and restaurants, international schools, the Obsidian spa and fitness cluster, food courts and creative spaces. No need to drive anywhere

Culture and events

Location
